I am a Machine Learning Engineer at [ViFive](https://vifive.co), where I build vision AI for musculoskeletal health. Think: pose estimation models that measure your range of motion as accurately as a doctor with a goniometer. We published the validation study with Stanford in the Journal of Medical Artificial Intelligence.

At ViFive, I've improved 3D pose accuracy by improving model architecture, designed a perspective-agnostic system that eliminated camera setup friction for users, and built temporal analysis pipelines that automated clinical assessments previously done by hand.

I'm the kind of person who builds things outside of work too. I recently shipped a [social app](/projects/6_project/) to see if I could take something from idea to launch. Turns out I could.

Before ViFive, I worked with the New York Mets on defensive analytics, building models that informed positioning strategy. Before that: MS in Data Science from USF, BA in Statistics from Berkeley, and a couple of internships back in Korea.

My current focus is computer vision and on-device ML, but I'm drawn to hard problems more than any single domain.
